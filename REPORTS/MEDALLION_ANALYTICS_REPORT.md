1. **Docker Containerization:** Multi-stage production container with dedicated non-root user (`uapuser:10001`).
2. **Kubernetes & Helm:** Automated scheduled ingestion via `batch/v1 CronJob` (every 6h) and persistent FastAPI daemon with Prometheus telemetry.
3. **Resilience & Deduplication:** Connection pooling, rotating User-Agents, deterministic SHA-256 content deduplication, and synthetic fallback circuit breaker.
